### New features

- The GUI's **View** menu gains **Bigger** (`Ctrl-=`), **Smaller** (`Ctrl--`), **Fit Window**
  (`Ctrl-0`) and **Snap Window**, which step the whole-number scale and resize the window to it,
  and an **Appearance** submenu (System, Light, Dark) for the GUI's own windows.
- `-scale N` and `-appearance system|light|dark`, in the GUI, saved by Save Changed Options.
